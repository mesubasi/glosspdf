Features

Real editing, not just viewing.

The tools you reach for every day, built into one clean window.

Mark up anything

Highlight, underline, strike out, add sticky notes, draw with the pen, place shapes and text boxes. Everything is saved as standard PDF annotations, so it looks the same in any viewer.

  • Adjustable highlight opacity
  • Comments sidebar for every note
  • Full undo and redo
Document tools

A full toolbox,
one click away.

Convert, compress, protect and redact without uploading a single page to someone else’s server.

Convert to Office

Turn a PDF into an editable Word document, an Excel workbook or a PowerPoint deck — right on your machine.

Privacy

Your documents never leave your disk.

Contracts, invoices, medical records — they stay on your computer. GlossPDF has no servers to send them to, and it works with your Wi-Fi switched off.
