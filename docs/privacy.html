GlossPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

GlossPDF is a PDF viewer and editor for Android. It works entirely on your device. We do not run any servers for the app, and we do not collect, store, share or sell your data.

Your documents

PDF files you open, edit, sign, merge, split, compress or protect are processed locally on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere. Edited files are saved only where you choose, or shared only with the app you pick in the Android share menu.

Data kept on your device

The app stores a short list of recently opened documents, your saved signatures and your theme and language settings in its private app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

GlossPDF does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or all of your files. It opens only the files you select through the Android file picker or send to it from another app.

Analytics, ads and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party accounts.

Children

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
